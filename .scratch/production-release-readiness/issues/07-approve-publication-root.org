* Requirements

- Review the provenance inventory and the absence of a trustworthy public
  boundary.

  content has complete license, authorship, and =tnsr= ownership evidence.

  history or metadata.

* Verification

Independent review confirms the approved root is exact, reproducible, and does
not silently discard or publish undispositioned work.

* Out of scope
