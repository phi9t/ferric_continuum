* Context

Not all pending work is implementation work. Some efforts are already complete
and need candidate-bound closure records. Others are paused on decisions about
assets, rootfs identity, B200 budgets, publication boundaries, archival
authority, or deletion. Those need supervision packets that ask for the missing
decision with enough evidence to answer it.

* Requirements

- Define the local file layout for consolidation artifacts, using one of:
  - ` .scratch/evidence-bound-agentic-engineering/receipts/ `;
  - ` .scratch/evidence-bound-agentic-engineering/supervision/ `;
  - source-effort-local `receipts/` and `supervision/` directories when the
    packet belongs more clearly beside its original spec.
- Record the selected layout in this ticket's `* Answer` and in
  `../pending-workstreams.org`.
- For completed efforts, write closure receipts that include:
  - source effort path;
  - final candidate commit or explicit candidate range when known;
  - acceptance claims;
  - evidence records or evidence gaps;
  - remaining risks;
  - promotion status distinct from completion status.
- Cover at least these completed efforts unless ticket 08 removes them:
  - `.scratch/incorporate-tnsr/`;
  - `.scratch/deepseek-v41-faithful-coverage/`;
  - `.scratch/tnsr-architecture-deepening/`;
  - `.scratch/typed-transformer-math/`;
  - `.scratch/desensitize-repo/`;
  - `.scratch/ferric-runner/`;
  - `.scratch/dtensor-mesh-simulation/`.
- For human-blocked efforts, write supervision packets that include:
  - decision needed;
  - why the current contract cannot decide it;
  - evidence already available;
  - options;
  - recommendation;
  - paused work and exact unblock effect.
- Cover at least these human-blocked decisions unless ticket 08 changes them:
  - DeepSeek V4.1 checkpoint asset admission;
  - DeepSeek V4.1 released-weight parity and final evidence packet;
  - production publication root approval;
  - self-contained rootfs information;
  - B200 qualification contract freeze;
  - standalone `tnsr` archive/delete authority.
- Do not copy raw prompts, credentials, full command logs, or unbounded source
  excerpts into receipts or supervision packets.

* Out of scope

- Resolving the human decisions.
- Rerunning expensive verification unless a receipt cannot be made truthful
  without a cheap current check.
- Deleting, archiving, pushing, merging, releasing, or publishing anything.
- Building an Emacs UI or task dashboard.

* Verification

- Every completed effort named by the ledger has either a closure receipt or a
  recorded reason no receipt can be produced yet.
- Every human-blocked effort named by the ledger has a supervision packet.
- Each packet separates completion, evidence, and promotion.
- Packets contain no raw trajectory content, credentials, or full logs.
- A reviewer can determine the next human decision without reading the full
  original conversation.
